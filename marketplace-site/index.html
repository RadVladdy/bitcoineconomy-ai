#Live from the relays snapshot

The differentiator: what actually announces itself on Nostr today, read from public relays (relay.damus.io, nos.lol, relay.primal.net) and probed live — Routstr inference providers (kind 38421) priced cheapest-first, ecash mints (NIP-87, kinds 38172/38173) with their signed reviews (kind 38000), and the self-announced sell side (kind 38555). These are announcements as published, not endorsements — and announcements outlive their nodes, so each clearnet endpoint is probed when the snapshot refreshes (dead ≠ delisted; onion-only endpoints can't be probed from here and show as unverified). Today's signed reviews target the mints, not the providers — verify a provider or mint before trusting it.

Self-announced services

The sell side: any service an agent can pay for can list itself here — no account, no fee — by publishing a signed agent-payable service announcement (our kind-38555 microstandard; Routstr's kind 38421 for inference). These are self-listed announcements, not endorsements: each clearnet endpoint is probed for liveness, and the trust signals to weigh are status, announcement age, and accepted-mint health. Announced ≠ curated — a service graduates into the curated registry (the other tab) only after editor verification. List your service →

#What listing actually means

Worth knowing before you fill anything in, because we would rather you list with your eyes open than be surprised by the label.

Announced ≠ curated. Your entry lands in the announced tier: published by you, taken as published, not endorsed by us. The directory shows it with its provenance and its probe status. A separate curated tier exists for entries an editor checked against primary sources — an announced entry graduates there only after that check, and only if it clears the API bar (an agent must be able to drive it through a real API).

We probe your endpoint and publish the answer. Every six hours your clearnet URL gets a plain GET. The result — alive, unreachable, unverified-tor-only, unroutable — is shown on your row. Dead is not delisted: if it stops answering we say so rather than quietly removing you.

It is on public relays, not on our server. We cannot edit it and we cannot delete it. To change or retire it, re-publish under the same d from the same key.
